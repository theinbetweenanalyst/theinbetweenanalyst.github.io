The In-Between Analyst

Short videos about life and craft as a Korean Business Analyst in Brisbane, Australia.

A YouTube channel: youtube.com/@theinbetweenanalyst
The channel's upload tool uses YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Most stories about Korean IT workers abroad are about developers. This channel shows the other seat at the table: the business analyst who sits between the people who use a system and the people who build it.

Each video is 30 to 45 seconds, with an English voiceover and Korean subtitles. The videos use an AI avatar and voice, and are labelled as AI-made on every platform.
